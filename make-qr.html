Video QR cards

Paste a YouTube link, give the card a name and add it to the sheet. Print the sheet and cut the cards out. Students scan them with the Watch icon on their iPad.

Some videos are set so they cannot play outside YouTube. If the check shows an error, pick a different video or ask the owner to allow embedding. The cards only work in the Watch icon, so scanning one with the normal Camera app shows plain text and opens nothing.
